I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), advised by [Prof. Jie Liu](https://drjieliu.github.io/). My research focuses on embodied intelligence, vision-language-action (VLA) models, world models, and efficient vision-language models for embodiment.

I am also a research intern at the International Digital Economy Academy (IDEA) and the Embodied Intelligence Laboratory of Guangdong Province (Futian Laboratory), where I am advised by [Prof. Lei Zhang](https://www.leizhang.org/) and work on full-stack embodied policy development, including robot and teleoperation platforms, data infrastructure, model pretraining and post-training, and evaluation.

Before beginning my Ph.D., I received a B.Eng. in Thermophysics from HIT's School of Energy Science and Engineering.

Research Interests
======

- Embodied intelligence and robot learning
- Vision-language-action models
- World models
- Efficient vision-language models for embodiment
